Ingredients
200 g, Chicken, Y
100 g, Basmati Rice, N
1 medium, Onion, Y
2 tbsp, Yogurt, Y
1 tsp, Biryani Masala, N
2 tbsp, Cooking Oil, N
Recipe
1. Wash and soak the rice for 20 minutes.
2. Slice the onion and fry it in cooking oil until golden brown.
3. Add the chicken, yogurt, and biryani masala. Cook until the chicken is tender.
4. Boil the rice separately until partially cooked.
5. Layer the rice over the chicken mixture and cook on low heat until fully done.
