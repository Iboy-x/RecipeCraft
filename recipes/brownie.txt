Ingredients
100 g, All-Purpose Flour, Y
1 medium, Egg, N
150 ml, Milk, Y
1 tbsp, Sugar, N
1 tsp, Baking Powder, N
1 tbsp, Butter, Y
Recipe
1. Add the flour, sugar, and baking powder to a bowl.
2. Add the egg and milk, then mix until a smooth batter forms.
3. Melt a little butter in a frying pan over medium heat.
4. Pour some batter into the pan and cook until bubbles appear.
5. Flip the pancake and cook the other side until golden brown.
